IT teams must ensure that software behaves consistently and can be updated without disruption, as delivering applications across organizations requires more than installation. Application packaging and deployment provide the structure needed to achieve this. Packaging prepares software for distribution, while deployment ensures it reaches the right systems in a controlled way.
