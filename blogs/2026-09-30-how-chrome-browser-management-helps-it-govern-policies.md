For many employees, Chrome is where most of the work gets done. It’s where they access SaaS apps, cloud storage, internal portals, AI tools, collaboration platforms, and other business systems they use almost every day. As more work moves into the browser, Chrome becomes another part of the enterprise environment that IT needs to manage.
