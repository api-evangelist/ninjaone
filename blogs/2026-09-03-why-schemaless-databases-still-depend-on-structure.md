The term “schemaless” is widely misunderstood. If you’ve ever set up a MongoDB collection or a DynamoDB table for the first time, you might think, “there’s no migrations, no common definitions, no upfront planning, just store data and move on. It’s freedom.” This thinking leads to the conclusion that schemaless = no structure.
