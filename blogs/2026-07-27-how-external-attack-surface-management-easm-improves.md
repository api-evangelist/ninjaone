Nowadays, more and more organizations are relying on cloud apps, SaaS backups, and shadow IT, expanding their attack surface. External Attack Surface Management (EASM) identifies, assesses, and secures parts of your infrastructure exposed to the internet for a wider range of security. Security policies focus on business-critical components; EASM blocks off the entry points.
