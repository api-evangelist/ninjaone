These days, most enterprise-level organizations operate across cloud platforms, SaaS applications, endpoint management systems, security tools, and distributed operational environments. As their infrastructures grow, operational data often becomes fragmented across disconnected systems and teams. These issues reduce operational trust and make it difficult to maintain accurate reporting, visibility, and accountability.
