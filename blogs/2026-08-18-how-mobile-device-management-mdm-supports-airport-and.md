Mobile device management (MDM) solutions have become a staple in modern environments, including airports. Most airports today use handheld devices for day-to-day work, from ground crews tracking baggage to gate agents managing onboarding. Because these environments need constant coordination and communication, they rely on MDM tools to keep their mobile devices secure and connected.
