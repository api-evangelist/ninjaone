When ransomware strikes, files can be encrypted, and critical business data may become inaccessible, disrupting normal operations. Minimizing downtime requires immediate containment and effective data protection, recovery measures, and a strong Microsoft 365 ransomware protection strategy. In this guide, you will learn how to quickly contain breaches, restore affected workloads, and prevent permanent data loss.
