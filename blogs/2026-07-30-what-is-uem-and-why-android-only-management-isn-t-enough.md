Android-only management refers to managing mobile devices, such as smartphones and tablets, typically through an Mobile Device Management (MDM) or Enterprise Mobility Management (EMM) platform. It helps IT teams enforce policies and monitor mobile endpoints from a central place. This setup works well if Android devices make up the entire device ecosystem.
