A 2025 Flexera report found that organizations overshoot their cloud budgets by 17% on average. On-premise deployments hide their own surprises: hardware refresh cycles, staffing overhead, and upgrade windows that vendor quotes rarely mention. When you’re evaluating cloud vs on-prem ITAM costs, neither option is priced honestly upfront.
