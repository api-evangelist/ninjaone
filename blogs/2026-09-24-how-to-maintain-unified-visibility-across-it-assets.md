Without unified IT asset visibility, your environment becomes prone to operational blind spots, such as inaccurate inventory records, licensing gaps, and weak audit readiness. This guide outlines a practical framework for maintaining continuous visibility across hardware, software, and distributed environments. It also covers the workflows needed to keep inventories accurate and lifecycle management on track.
