For the first time, UK managed service providers (MSPs) are about to become regulated entities. Until now, the cyber obligations you dealt with were really your clients, and you met them because a customer contract or security questionnaire required it. The Cyber Security and Resilience Bill (Network and Information Systems) will change that.
