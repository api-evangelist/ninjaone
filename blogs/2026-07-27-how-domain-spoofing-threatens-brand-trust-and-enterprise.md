Domain spoofing is a cyberattack technique where bad actors impersonate a legitimate domain to deceive users, customers, or employees. By exploiting weaknesses in domain authentication or registering lookalike domains, attackers create convincing fraudulent communications and websites. Unlike DNS spoofing or IP spoofing, domain spoofing targets trust in brand identity rather than network infrastructure.
